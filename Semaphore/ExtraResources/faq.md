### Q: What does Semaphore do?
A: Semaphore lets you build a simple sequence of workout timers with optional GIF references, then play them one by one or run the full routine from start to finish.

### Q: Can I run a full routine automatically?
A: Yes. Tap the main play button on the workout list to run the entire routine in order. Semaphore advances to the next timer automatically when the current one finishes.

### Q: Will the timer keep going if I leave the app?
A: Yes. Semaphore keeps the routine running based on the timer schedule, continues playback when you return, and uses iPhone notifications to alert you when a timer ends and the next one begins.

### Q: What shows up on the Lock Screen while a timer is running?
A: Semaphore can show the active workout timer outside the app, along with the current workout image when one is available. GIF-based workouts use a still preview image in system surfaces where animation is not supported.

### Q: Why am I not seeing notifications or the Lock Screen timer?
A: Make sure notifications are allowed for Semaphore and that Live Activities are enabled for the app in iPhone settings. If permission was denied the first time, you may need to re-enable it manually in Settings.

### Q: Why do some workouts show an image and others do not?
A: Workout images are optional. If you attach a GIF or image while creating or editing a workout, Semaphore uses it as a visual reference during playback and in notifications when available.

### Q: Why are my stats not changing?
A: Stats update when a workout timer fully completes. Stopping early will not count as a finished workout.

### Q: Can I edit a workout while another timer is running?
A: You can open workouts while playback is active, but changing or deleting workouts that are part of the current routine may stop the active timer so the schedule stays accurate.

### Q: How do I send feedback?
A: Open Settings and tap `Send Feedback` to start an email with the app's support address.
